Project option: classic-games
Problem: I wanted to create a simple, fun classic game that gives two players an accessible and fair way to compete together in the browser without requiring accounts, downloads, or a backend.
What I built: A responsive two-player Pop the Balloons reflex game. Each player gets a 60-second turn to pop balloons; the highest score wins. The game supports keyboard play, pause/resume, adaptive layouts, optional generated sound, and stores the all-time high score locally in the browser.
Result: A complete, mobile-friendly two-player browser game that works from a single HTML file. Players can enter their names, take timed turns, see the winner and final scores, replay immediately, and keep an all-time high score on their device.
